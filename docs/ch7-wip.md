# Chapter 7, The Plumbers: work in progress (handoff)

Chapter 7 is being built in the HD cartoon look (GAME_DESIGN.md, "Act 3" and "Chapter 7: The Plumbers"). It is **not finished**: release builds show it as COMING SOON and can't start it by URL (`levels/chapters.ts` `CH7_BUILT`, `chapters/registry.ts` `knownLevelIds`); dev and bench builds play it. Remove both gates when the chapter is done. Chapters 1 to 6 must stay exactly as they are (the owner's rule): everything new is chapter-specific or only switches on in an HD presentation.

## How to look at it (dev server)

- `?level=ch7&mute=1&god&at=<tile x>`: play from any tile (e.g. 40 the sentries, 74 the flooded ring, 120 the valve, 160 the reactor, 200 the open sea, 276 the pump hall, 360 containment, 414 the moon pool: the fight starts at once).
- `?level=ch7&start=cp-drydock` / `cp-maelstrom`: Deepmaw from his second or third stage. Headless Chromium runs the game slowly, so walking in from `cp-moonpool` takes several seconds of held input.
- `?level=ch7&start=cp-sea` etc.: start at a checkpoint (the base resumes in the right state).
- `scripts/cutout/poses.cjs` and `scripts/cutout/capture.cjs` (dev server running, `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g)`): characters posed on white in WebGL, and a frame-perfect gameplay recording.
- A rig's poses on white, true colours: a page script through the dev server that imports `cutout/cutout.ts` (await `loadCutouts`), `cast.ts`, `rig.ts` and `paint.ts`, poses with `Pose`, `applyFace` and `solve`, and draws each part at its bone.
- `?level=ch7&rigview=ben,ripjaws&zoom=4&cols=6&cellh=46&artres=10`: the rig viewer (`artres` paints the art sharper for close review; dev builds only). The level's lighting tints it: for true colours, render the rig's parts straight to a canvas (import `cast.ts`, `rig.ts` and `paint.ts` through the dev server, pose with `Pose`, `solve` and draw each part at its bone). Known quirk: with `clips=` it sometimes leaves actors in the rest pose; posing actors by hand in a page script (create `RigActor`s, `play`, `update`) shows every clip reliably.

## Gotchas

- Per-frame work goes through `systems/Pacing.ts`; no allocations in `update()` paths; lights via `lighting.add` per frame; clean up on SHUTDOWN.
- `setCrop` is wrong on HD textures (their frames are normalised to game pixels while the crop is in texels): doors slide behind the terrain instead.
- Walkers die in level water (`mutants/kit.walkPhysics`), but Chapter 7's water is tanks, not level water, so the grunt walks tank floors.
- `DeepKind` uses `warden` for the sentry (`sentry` is Chapter 5's crystal sentry; animation keys would clash).
