# Chapter 7, The Plumbers: work in progress (handoff)

Chapter 7 is being built in the HD cartoon look (GAME_DESIGN.md, "Act 3" and "Chapter 7: The Plumbers"). It is **not finished**: release builds show it as COMING SOON and can't start it by URL (`levels/chapters.ts` `CH7_BUILT`, `chapters/registry.ts` `knownLevelIds`); dev and bench builds play it. ## How to look at it (dev server)

- `?level=ch7&mute=1&god&at=<tile x>`: play from any tile (e.g. 40 the sentries, 74 the flooded ring, 120 the valve, 160 the reactor, 200 the open sea, 276 the pump hall, 360 containment, 420 the moon pool).
- `?level=ch7&start=cp-sea` etc.: start at a checkpoint (the base resumes in the right state).
- `?level=ch7&rigview=ben,ripjaws&zoom=4&cols=6&cellh=46&artres=10`: the rig viewer (`artres` paints the art sharper for close review; dev builds only). The level's lighting tints it: for true colours, render the rig's parts straight to a canvas (import `cast.ts`, `rig.ts` and `paint.ts` through the dev server, pose with `Pose`, `solve` and draw each part at its bone). Known quirk: with `clips=` it sometimes leaves actors in the rest pose; posing actors by hand in a page script (create `RigActor`s, `play`, `update`) shows every clip reliably.

## Gotchas

- Per-frame work goes through `systems/Pacing.ts`; no allocations in `update()` paths; lights via `lighting.add` per frame; clean up on SHUTDOWN.
- `setCrop` is wrong on HD textures (their frames are normalised to game pixels while the crop is in texels): doors slide behind the terrain instead.
- Walkers die in level water (`mutants/kit.walkPhysics`), but Chapter 7's water is tanks, not level water, so the grunt walks tank floors.
- `DeepKind` uses `warden` for the sentry (`sentry` is Chapter 5's crystal sentry; animation keys would clash).
